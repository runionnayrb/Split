Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 3, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information SplitMate collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have designed SplitMate from the ground up to minimize data collection and keep your financial information where it belongs — with you.

1. Information We Collect

SplitMate is designed with a privacy-first architecture. Here is a complete picture of what we do and do not collect:

What we do NOT collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials (no accounts are required)
  • Your transaction history, expense amounts, or balance data — all of this lives in your browser's local storage only
  • Your payment information, bank account details, or Zelle/Venmo credentials
  • Any personal identifiers or tracking cookies across sessions

What may touch our server:

  • Receipt images — when you use the Scan Receipt feature, the image or PDF you upload is transmitted to our server solely to be forwarded to OpenAI's API for processing. The image is not stored on our server after the response is returned.
  • Standard server logs — like any web server, we may log basic request information (IP address, timestamp, URL accessed) for operational and security purposes. These logs are rotated regularly and are not used for advertising or profiling.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All of your expense data — transactions, partner names, and settings — is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device under normal use
  • Clearing your browser's cache or storage will permanently delete your data
  • Data is not synced between devices or browsers
  • We cannot access, retrieve, or restore your local data if it is lost

3. Receipt Scanning & OpenAI

When you upload a receipt for AI scanning, the following happens:

  • The image is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our server
  • Our server forwards it to the OpenAI API (GPT-4o Vision) to extract expense data
  • The extracted data is returned to your browser and displayed for your review
  • The receipt image is not persisted on our server after the API response is returned

OpenAI may retain submitted content per their own data policies. We encourage you to review OpenAI's Privacy Policy for more detail. Avoid uploading receipts containing sensitive personal information beyond standard purchase details.

4. Cookies & Tracking

SplitMate does not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics), or any cross-site tracking technology. The App may use browser localStorage and sessionStorage to maintain your settings and preferences within your own device. No cookies are set for the purpose of tracking you across the web.

5. Third-Party Services

SplitMate integrates with the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI — for receipt scanning. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third parties for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

SplitMate is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, no personal data is collected and no action is needed.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect any data that passes through our server, including encrypted HTTPS connections and not persisting receipt images. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf. To delete your data, simply clear your browser's local storage — your transactions and settings will be permanently removed from your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of SplitMate after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the app. We take privacy seriously and will respond to all reasonable inquiries.
