✦ FAQ

Questions & answers

Everything you're probably wondering before you try it.

Is SplitMate really free? ▾

Yes — SplitMate is completely free during our early access period. No credit card, no trial, no hidden fees. We'll give you plenty of notice before anything changes.

How does AI receipt scanning work? ▾

You upload a photo, screenshot, or PDF of any receipt. SplitMate sends it to OpenAI's GPT-4o Vision model, which reads every line item, price, tax, tip, and fee. The results come back in a few seconds as a structured list you can review and approve before anything is saved.

Is my financial data private? ▾

Your transaction history lives entirely in your browser's local storage — it never leaves your device and is never stored on our servers. The only data that touches our server is a receipt image you choose to upload for scanning, and that is passed directly to OpenAI and not retained. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Does it work on iPhone? ▾

Absolutely. SplitMate is fully optimized for iPhone with a native-feeling interface, touch-friendly controls, and iOS safe-area support. Open it in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen to use it like a real app — no App Store download required.

Can I use Venmo or Cash App instead of Zelle? ▾

Of course. The app shows a Zelle number for quick copying, but you can settle up through any payment method you prefer — Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or even cash. SplitMate just tracks the balance; how you transfer money is entirely up to you.

Can I edit a transaction after adding it? ▾

Yes. Tap any transaction to open its detail view, then tap Edit. You can change the description, amount, category, who paid, and the date. Changes save instantly and the balance updates automatically.

What if we share the app across two phones? ▾

Right now each browser instance has its own local data, so one partner enters expenses and the other can view the shareable receipt images. Full real-time sync between devices is on our roadmap. For now, the easiest workflow is one person manages the app and settles up when the balance is ready.
