One Card. Fair Charges.

Share one virtual card for purchases — each partner links their real card and only gets charged exactly what they owe.

💳
One shared virtual card
You get a single virtual card to use together for shared purchases — groceries, dining, subscriptions, anything. Add it to Apple Pay or shop online.
🏦
Each partner links their real card
You and your partner each connect your own debit or credit card. SplitMate uses them to settle charges — no shared bank account needed.
⚖️
Each person pays only their share
When the virtual card is charged, SplitMate automatically splits the amount and charges each partner's real card for only what they owe — 50/50 or any ratio you set.
⚡
No IOUs, no chasing people down
Every purchase is settled the moment it happens. No more tracking who paid last or sending Zelle requests — it's all automatic.
